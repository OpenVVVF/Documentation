# Safety and Compliance

Cross-cutting safety and compliance documentation. HARA Core applies to the control module and every power stage built from it. Application profiles provide example risk assessments for specific vehicle classes. [Testing and Validation](../Testing/Index.md) provides the evidence that mitigations and requirements were exercised.

- **HARA** - Hazard Analysis and Risk Assessment
- **TARA** - Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment (ISO/SAE 21434 cybersecurity)
- **Compliance** - Standards mapping (ISO 26262, IEC 61800-5-2)

Formal test reports and validation evidence live in the top-level [Testing](../Testing/Index.md) section, not under this section.
