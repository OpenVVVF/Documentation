Proven on real hardware

Chassis Size 2 (~100 kW class) has run a 180 V bring-up, a 1-hour endurance run, a 20-minute reversal stress test, and motor self-commissioning — 10 formal test reports. Next: a Size 2 chassis for our 2018 Zero DSR electric motorcycle.

Safety-engineered

Designed like it matters

Dual-MCU architecture with six redundant safe-state pathways; HARA + FMEA with ISO 26262 and IEC 61800-5-2 mapping; every hazard traces to a safety requirement and a fault-injection test.

Open everything

Nothing sealed, nothing hidden

Schematics, firmware, the RTE Studio model-based workflow (no MATLAB required; ADRC & MPCC benchmarked against FOC), safety analyses, and interactive telemetry logs — all public at docs.openvvvf.org.
