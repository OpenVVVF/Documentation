power-conversion platform

One reusable dual-MCU control module. Four power-stage chassis spanning approximately 50 kW to the megawatt class. Motor drives and bidirectional DC-DC conversion, with IGBTs today and SiC-ready designs. Every schematic, line of firmware, safety analysis, and test trace is published.

Built and bench-tested

Size 2 (approximately 100 kW) has completed a 180 V bring-up, a one-hour endurance run, a 20-minute reversal stress test, and instrument-verified motor self-commissioning. Next, a Size 2 chassis is being integrated into our 2018 Zero DSR electric motorcycle.

Safety engineered

Dual-MCU architecture with six redundant safe-state pathways. The hazard analysis (HARA) and FMEA map to ISO 26262 and IEC 61800-5-2. Every hazard traces to a safety requirement and a test.

Open everything

Schematics, firmware, the RTE Studio model-based workflow (no MATLAB; alternative control algorithms benchmarked against FOC), safety analyses, and interactive telemetry logs are all public at docs.openvvvf.org.
