Overview

The open, safety-engineered
power-conversion platform

Built on our bench-tested dual-MCU control architecture. Swappable power stages scale from 10 kW to the megawatt class for motor drives and DC/DC conversion, with SiC-ready designs.

Built and bench-tested

Size 2 has completed a 180 V bring-up, a one-hour endurance run, a 20-minute reversal stress test, and instrument-verified motor self-commissioning. The Size 2 chassis is being tested for use on a 2018 Zero DSR electric motorcycle.

Safety engineered

Six independent paths to the safe state combine hardware shutdown with main-controller and safety-coprocessor authority. The HARA and FMEA map to ISO 26262 and IEC 61800-5-2, with hazards traced to safety requirements and validation.

Open everything

Schematics, firmware, safety analyses, assembly data, interactive telemetry, and RTE Studio, an open-source model-based development and code-generation tool, are public at docs.openvvvf.org. Sponsors and researchers can inspect what was built and how it performed.
